Questions, answered.

أسئلة بتتسأل كتير
How do I know my sizes are right?

The guide shows where to place the tape for each of the 8 measurements. Before cutting, the tailor checks your sizes by call. For a first order that can be a video call or a WhatsApp check.

الأسطى بيراجع المقاسات معاك بمكالمة قبل القص.
How long does it take?

About 14 days. Every piece is cut and sewn by hand.

حوالي ١٤ يوم.
What if it does not fit?

You get one free adjustment if the fit is off.

تعديل مجاني مرة واحدة لو المقاس مش مظبوط.
How do I pay?

A deposit of 30–50% confirms the order and the rest is paid on delivery. The tailor confirms the exact amount on WhatsApp.

عربون من ٣٠ لـ ٥٠٪ والباقي عند الاستلام.
What do silk lining, embroidery and extra pockets cost?

The base jacket is 1,300 EGP. Details are added on top, and the tailor sets each price once the final figures are agreed.

أسعار الإضافات بتتحدد مع الأسطى.
Can you copy a piece from a photo?

Yes. Send a clear photo and tell us the piece and your sizes. The tailor looks at it and replies with a price and a delivery time.

ابعت صورة والأسطى يرد عليك بالسعر والمدة.
Why is there a waiting list?

One master tailor does the work, so the number of orders each week is limited.

الشغل على إيد أسطى واحد، فعدد الطلبات في الأسبوع محدود.
